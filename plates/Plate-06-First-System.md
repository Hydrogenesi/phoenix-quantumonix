## II. Cosmogenic Context

P01-P05 establish the atomic elements: direction, motion, distinction, fields, and individual interactions.
P06 is the moment these atomic elements **organize into systems**.

The First System is not merely a collection of interactions. It is a structure where:
- Multiple interactions are **coupled** and interdependent
- The system maintains **internal coherence** despite external perturbation
- The system has **emergent properties** that cannot be reduced to single interactions
- The system **persists** through time by regenerating its own components

This is the birth of:
- Networks
- Organization
- Stability through complexity
- Emergent behavior
- System identity

Without P06, the Codex remains a universe of isolated interactions. With P06, it becomes capable of **life-like behavior**.

---

## III. The Nucleus of P06

The Nucleus of P06 consists of three organizational invariants:

**S₁: Internal Coupling**
A system must have interactions that couple its components together.
- No component may be truly isolated
- Perturbation in one region must propagate to others
- Coupling must preserve the continuity spine (P01)

**S₂: Emergent Coherence**
A system must exhibit behaviors and properties that arise from the organization,
not from any single component.
- The whole must be demonstrably different from the sum of parts
- System-level properties must be traceable back to component interactions
- Coherence must be maintainable even if components are individually unstable

**S₃: Self-Maintenance**
A system must be capable of regenerating or restoring itself.
- Components may be consumed or lost
- The system must have mechanisms to replace or repair
- Self-maintenance must not violate P03's boundary integrity or P05's interaction laws

These three invariants ensure that P06 systems remain coherent, coupled, and persistent
without breaking upstream plates.
