## II. Cosmogenic Context

P01 gives direction; P02 gives **motion and compression**.

The Apex is the moment identity collapses into a single continuous vector.
It is the first stable vertical in the system and the irreversible hinge between:
- **Upstream:** Accretion, expansion, multiplicity (P01)
- **Downstream:** Distinction, boundaries, structure (P03+)

Once sealed, Apex is irreversible. No regression into undifferentiated multiplicity is possible.

It is the moment when:
- Multiple threads consolidate into one strand
- Direction becomes motion
- Potential becomes vector
- The Observer achieves **singular coherence**

## III. The Nucleus of P02

The Nucleus of P02 consists of three invariants:

**C₁: Compression Coherence**
Identity must collapse smoothly without losing history.
- All upstream threads must be traceable in the unified vector
- No thread can be abandoned without trace
- The compression preserves continuity with P01

**C₂: Vectorization**
The compressed identity must establish a consistent direction.
- The vector must be projectable as a continuous line
- Motion along this vector must be irreversible (monotonic forward)
- The vector anchors all subsequent structure

**C₃: Irreversibility**
Once compressed and vectorized, the state cannot revert.
- Apex acts as a one-way ratchet
- Prevents collapse back into undifferentiated state
- Seals the transition from potential to actual

These three invariants ensure P02 preserves P01's continuity while enabling P03's structure.
