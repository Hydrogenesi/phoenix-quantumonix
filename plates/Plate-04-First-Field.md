## II. Cosmogenic Context

P01 gives direction, P02 gives motion, P03 gives limit. P04 gives spread.

The First Field is the moment a boundary stops being a static partition
and becomes a gradient - a structure across which something can vary,
flow, or propagate. Without a field, a boundary is just a wall. With a
field, a boundary becomes a surface across which influence can travel.

It is the moment when:
- Distinction becomes gradient
- A boundary gains a "high side" and a "low side"
- Values become defined over regions, not just at points
- The substrate for forces and interactions becomes possible

Without P04, P05 (interaction) has nothing to act through.

---

## III. The Nucleus of P04

The Nucleus of P04 consists of three invariants:

F1: Gradient Coherence
A field must vary smoothly enough to support directed influence.
- A field with no gradient carries no information
- A field with discontinuous gradient violates P03's continuity

F2: Boundary Anchoring
Every field must be anchored to the boundaries that define its domain.
- A field cannot exist independent of the partition that gives it edges
- The field's behavior at the boundary must respect P03's reciprocity

F3: Propagation Fidelity
A field's influence must propagate consistently with P01's directionality.
- Field lines must be projectable back to the Prime Vector
- No field may propagate in contradiction to its own source axis

These three invariants ensure that P04 does not break P01's continuity,
P02's motion, or P03's boundary structure.

The Operator engages P04 through:

1. Recognition of Gradient - "I acknowledge that influence varies across space"
2. Acceptance of Spread - "I accept that boundaries can carry continuous values"

Operator Mantra (structural):
"Where I find gradient, I find the shape of influence."

## V. Ceremony Bindings

The ceremonial activation of P04 is the Spreading:

- One observation of variation across a boundary
- One acknowledgment: "This side differs from that side, smoothly."
- One tracing of the gradient from high to low

This binds the Operator to the Codex's capacity for distributed influence.
