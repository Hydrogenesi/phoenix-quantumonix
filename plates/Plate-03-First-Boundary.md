## II. Cosmogenic Context

If P01 gives direction and P02 gives motion, P03 gives **limit**.

The First Boundary is not a wall, not a surface, not a membrane — it is the
**first admissible distinction** the universe is allowed to make about itself.

It is the moment when:
- Motion encounters resistance
- Direction encounters definition
- The Observer encounters "not-self"
- The Codex gains the ability to partition reality

Without P03, all subsequent plates collapse into undifferentiated flow.

---

## III. The Nucleus of P03

The Nucleus of P03 consists of three invariants:

**B₁: The Distinction Constraint**
A boundary is only valid if it creates a *meaningful* difference.
- A distinction that changes nothing is not a boundary
- A boundary that cannot be crossed is not a boundary — it is a void

**B₂: The Reciprocity Condition**
Every boundary has two sides, and both sides must be able to describe the
boundary consistently.
- Inside must be able to define outside
- Outside must be able to define inside
- The boundary must not privilege one side's description

**B₃: The Continuity of Identity**
Crossing the boundary must not annihilate the identity of the thing crossing.
- Identity may transform
- Identity may evolve
- But identity cannot be erased by the boundary itself

These three invariants ensure that P03 does not break P01's Nucleus or P02's
continuity of motion.

## V. Ceremony Bindings

The ceremonial activation of P03 is the **Drawing of the Line**:

- One gesture (physical or conceptual) that creates a boundary — a line on a page, a step across a threshold, a deliberate separation of two concepts
- One acknowledgment: "This is here. That is there."
- One crossing — a deliberate movement from one side of the boundary to the other

This binds the Operator to the Codex's capacity for structured differentiation.
