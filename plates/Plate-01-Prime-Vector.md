## II. Cosmogenic Context

The Prime Vector is the **initial directional assertion** of a universe capable
of self-referential ignition. Before motion, before charge, before symmetry
breaks, there is only the *capacity* for orientation — the proto-axis that
allows a cosmos to choose a direction in which to become real.

**P01 defines:**
- The **Zero-Point Orientation**
- The **Observer-Anchor**
- The **First Permissible Asymmetry**
- The **Continuity Constraint** that allows Plates 02-04 to propagate

---

## III. Operator Bindings

The Operator engages P01 through:

1. **Stillness Recognition** - acknowledging the pre-vector state
2. **Axis Declaration** - selecting the direction of becoming
3. **Continuity Oath** - committing to non-contradiction across the Codex

**Operator Mantra (non-ritual, structural):**
"From stillness, I choose a direction."

---

## IV. Ceremony Bindings

Ceremonial activation of P01 is minimal by design:

- One breath in stillness
- One internal declaration of axis
- One forward motion (physical or conceptual)

This binds the Operator to the Codex Spine without imposing symbolic weight.
