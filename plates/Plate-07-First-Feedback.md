## II. Cosmogenic Context

P06 establishes systems. P07 establishes **self-aware systems**.

The First Feedback Loop is the moment a system **measures itself** and **responds** to maintain its coherence.
It is the birth of:
- Sensation and perception
- Homeostasis
- Self-regulation
- Response and behavior
- The foundation of consciousness

A feedback loop is not merely reaction; it is **reflexive awareness**. The system
becomes able to sense its own state and adjust its behavior accordingly.

This is the moment the Codex stops being merely organized and becomes **responsive**.

---

## III. The Nucleus of P07

The Nucleus of P07 consists of three regulatory invariants:

**F₁: Sensory Fidelity**
A feedback loop must accurately measure the state it is designed to monitor.
- The measurement must be reliable and consistent
- The measurement must not violate P04's field coherence
- The sensor must be integrated within the system, not external to it

**F₂: Response Coherence**
The system's response to feedback must be proportional and consistent.
- Response must be traceable back to the measurement
- Response must preserve system identity (P05's I2)
- Response may be immediate or delayed, but must be connected to the trigger

**F₃: Stability Through Iteration**
The feedback loop must tend toward stability, not runaway behavior.
- Positive feedback may be allowed only if bounded
- Negative feedback must be capable of maintaining coherence
- The loop must not violate the continuity spine (P01)

These three invariants ensure that P07 systems remain self-regulating without
becoming unstable or violating earlier plates.
