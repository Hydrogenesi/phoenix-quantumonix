## II. Cosmogenic Context

P01-P04 prepare the stage: P01 gives direction, P02 gives motion, P03 gives
distinction, P04 gives fields. But none of these alone produce interaction.
A universe with fields but no interactions is a silent geometry.

P05 is the first moment two structures exchange influence. It is the birth of:
- Force
- Communication
- Causality
- Agency
- Event

This is the plate where the Codex stops being a static architecture and
becomes a living system.

---

## III. The Nucleus of P05

The Nucleus of P05 consists of three relational invariants:

I1: Mutual Influence
Interaction must be bidirectional, even if asymmetric.
If A affects B, B must in some way affect A, even if only by altering
the context. This preserves P03's reciprocity and P01's continuity.

I2: Conservation of Identity
Interaction may transform entities, but cannot annihilate identity
without trace. This ensures:
- No interaction breaks the continuity spine
- No event erases the history that produced it

I3: Event Integrity
An interaction is a single coherent event if:
- It has a definable beginning and end
- It produces a measurable change
- It is projectable back to P01's Prime Vector

This prevents paradoxical or non-causal interactions.

2. Consent to Exchange - "I allow influence to pass between myself and the world"

## V. Ceremony Bindings - The Exchange Gesture

The ceremonial activation of P05 is simple but profound:

- One Offering - a gesture, word, or action directed outward
- One Reception - a willingness to be changed by what returns
- One Integration - acknowledging the event as part of your continuity

This binds the Operator to the relational fabric of the Codex.
